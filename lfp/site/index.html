H1 · Basin shape

Filter-normalized 1-D slices through the final weights, \(L(\theta + a\,d) - L(\theta)\), averaged over directions. Line = median run, band = min–max across runs in the group. Narrower and bumpier = sharper, rougher basin.

H2 · Where the two losses fight

Three per-layer scores, median across runs. Read them together: the cosine alone is not a pinch-point test (see Info).

Direction · \(c_\ell = \cos(\nabla \mathrm{BCE}, \nabla \mathrm{DisCo})\)

Orange = the terms pull this layer in opposite directions, cyan = the same direction. At a converged trade-off the cosine is near \(-1\) by construction, so orange in the final column alone means equilibrium, not a defect.

Balance · \(\rho_\ell = \alpha\lVert\nabla \mathrm{DisCo}\rVert / \lVert\nabla \mathrm{BCE}\rVert\)

Gray = the two weighted pulls are comparable (\(\rho \approx 1\)), so their direction matters. Cyan = BCE dominates, orange = DisCo dominates. Colour is \(\log_{10}\rho\), labels are \(\rho\); \(\alpha = 0\) runs read 0.

Cancellation · \(\kappa_\ell = \lVert\nabla \mathrm{BCE} + \alpha\nabla \mathrm{DisCo}\rVert / (\lVert\nabla \mathrm{BCE}\rVert + \alpha\lVert\nabla \mathrm{DisCo}\rVert)\)

\(\kappa\) near 0 = the pulls cancel and the layer barely moves despite large gradients; 1 = they add. ◆ marks a provisional stall (\(\kappa < 0.3\) and \(1/3 < \rho < 3\)). A pinch-point candidate is a layer stalled during training (epochs 5–20) in most seeds, more than the other layers.

H3 · Curvature over training

Largest (\(\lambda_{\max}\)) and most negative (\(\lambda_{\min}\)) Hessian eigenvalue of the total loss on the probe batch. Thick = group median, thin = individual runs. Checkpoint-only runs show the final point only.

H4 · Seed-pair barriers

Loss bump above the straight line between two seeds' weights. Hidden units can be permuted without changing the network, so compare groups of pairs with each other, not with zero. Prediction distance (right) is permutation-free.

Minibatch vs full-sample dCor

DisCo is a batch statistic. Points above the diagonal: training saw more dependence per minibatch than exists in the full sample (small-sample bias); the gap shrinks as the DisCo batch grows.

Fingerprint vs metric exploratory

Any fingerprint feature against any ledger metric. Looking here is not the kill test: that runs once, pre-registered, on held-out data.
